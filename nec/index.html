02 — Course Structure

A complete, navigable unit, not a skeleton.

The course is organised into clearly labelled sections with a persistent sidebar so learners always know where they are. Completion indicators give real-time progress feedback as learners work through the unit.

Design note: The sidebar structure mirrors the kind of navigation pattern NEC learners will recognise: section headings, nested activity titles, and completion status visible at a glance. The intention was to make the experience feel familiar from the first click.

04 — Quiz Design

Adaptive, conditional, and learner-centred.

The Unit 1 quiz uses 5 questions across three formats (multiple choice, matching, and select missing words), with an 80% pass threshold, a 3-attempt limit, and conditional feedback that responds differently depending on whether the learner passes or fails.

Design note: Three attempts and then a "revisit the unit" consequence, not unlimited retries. Learners who have not passed after three attempts should go back to the material, not keep clicking through. That felt like the right call for this kind of self-paced distance learning context.
